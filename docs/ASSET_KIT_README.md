# Bubble Arena — 2007-era water-balloon arcade visual reference kit

**This is not a copy of the proprietary Boom Online 2007 asset archive.** It combines AI-generated visual reference sheets and distinct, original, individually usable prototype PNG/WAV art and sounds. Do not treat the generated board as historically authentic pixel-for-pixel artwork or use its text labels as specifications.

## Visual design target
- **Perspective:** orthographic, top-down, square-grid arena. No artillery shots or side-on terrain.
- **Look:** saturated cobalt/cyan panels, orange/yellow action buttons, rounded chibi figures, dark outlines, gentle highlights and playful 2000s PC-game UI.
- **Gameplay visual:** rounded blue water balloons create a four-directional + water blast that stops at walls; weaker crates can be removed.
- **Prototype artwork:** 64 × 64 transparent sprites per file. Intended as a game scaffold, not final production drawings. They are drawn independently from original proprietary characters.
- **Visual reference sheets:** 00_reference_boards — concept boards generated for the requested old-school aesthetic.
- **Audio:** 10 original .wav sounds including a basic instrumental lobby loop. These are synthetic reference sounds and NOT original Boom Online sound files.

## Inventory
- Characters: 8 distinct chibi avatars.
- Floors: 8 square tile types.
- Obstacles: 10 crate / structure / decorative types.
- Powerups: 9 icons.
- Water bombs: 4 inflation frames.
- Water blasts: 6 animated visual frames.
- UI: 4 Vietnamese action buttons.
- Audio: 11 original WAV assets including music.
- Previews: 2 assembled example screens / sprite catalog.

## Priorities for a browser remake
1. Implement water-blast tile rules, character movement and enemy trapping before polish.
2. Render the entire fixed-timestep playfield at a consistent logical resolution; scale responsively without changing tile-hit geometry.
3. Use distinct mobile layouts (not scaled-down desktop UI); keep controls outside playable cells.
4. Revisit visual effects and custom art after mechanics are stable.
